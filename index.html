02 — Selected work

First-author papers

IEEE/ACM MICRO 2026 Best Artifact Honorable Mention

QROB

Quantifying Realization Overhead in Quantum Compilation via Reverse Construction

A reverse-construction methodology that generates compilation instances backward from directly realizable configurations, keeping the inverse paths as feasible, compiler-independent references — for NISQ SWAP routing, lattice-surgery scheduling and quantum memory access.

arXiv Artifact
24.1×reference SWAP cost incurred by NISQ compilers, at most
7.0×reference makespan required by fault-tolerant compilers, at most
84.8%of real-world circuits where a QROB-trained router outperforms Qiskit SABRE
1.65×median mirror-circuit survival vs. Qiskit O3 on 156-qubit IBM Heron r2
ICML 2026 PMLR 306

LoRe

Adaptive Interaction-Evaluation Routing with Per-step Interaction Budgets for Iterative Graph Solvers

Adaptive per-step interaction budgets that make learning-based iterative graph solvers faster and more memory-efficient, without retraining the base model.

Proceedings arXiv ICML page
≈8×faster inference on Maximum Independent Set
≈12×lower peak memory on Maximum Independent Set
≈15×speedup on TSP with 1,000 nodes
44×memory reduction on TSP with 1,000 nodes
Honor · MICRO 2026

Best Artifact Honorable Mention

Awarded to QROB at the 59th IEEE/ACM International Symposium on Microarchitecture — one of 3 awards among 94 evaluated artifacts, with Available, Reviewed and Reproducible badges.
